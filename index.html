FitMax

A body analysis from three photos, and a training programme built on it

Take a front, a side and a back photo. FitMax scores your physique out of 100 — overall, muscle, aesthetics, symmetry and potential — estimates your body-fat percentage, and places a score on each of six muscle groups, drawn over your own photo. From that report it writes a weekly training programme, and every new analysis is compared with the last one.

How an analysis works

  • Three photos, fitted clothes, good light. Camera or gallery. The phone measures the skeleton on the device first — shoulder-to-waist ratio, tilt, sharpness — so the analysis has objective numbers to hold onto, not just an impression.
  • The photos and your profile are read together. Age, sex, height, weight, goal and training history give the scores context: the same shoulders mean something different on a beginner and on someone with five years of training.
  • One report, and the reasons under it. Six headline numbers, a muscle map over your photo, what is already strong, what will grow fastest, what to focus on. The wording is fixed to be encouraging: an observation always comes with the next step.
  • A programme that follows from it. Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, full body — chosen from your report, your days and your equipment, with an exercise library that shows which muscle each movement loads.

What it deliberately does not do

There is no account and no social layer. Photos are not stored on any server. Nothing is compared with other people's bodies, and no report is ever shown to anyone but you unless you share it yourself.

FitMax Premium

Every analysis and every generated programme is an AI call, so those open with FitMax Premium. It is sold weekly or monthly; the store shows the price and the period in your own currency at the moment you subscribe, and those are the terms that apply. There is no free trial at the time of writing. The subscription renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel. The exercise library, workout logging and your saved reports stay open without it.

Languages

Ten: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Indonesian and Arabic. A report is written in the language the app is set to when you run it.

FitMax is not a medical device. Its scores and its body-fat figure are estimates from photographs, made for motivation and training guidance. They do not diagnose, treat or measure anything, and they are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet or training because of a number.
