Delete your data

App: FitMax · iOS bundle: com.fitmax.sameh · Android package: com.fitmax.sameh

There is no account to delete

FitMax never creates one. There is no sign-in, no email address and no user record on a server. Everything the app knows about you is on your phone, and you remove it yourself.

On your phone

  • Settings → Delete my data — removes every analysis with its photos, your profile, your programmes and your workout log in one step, after a confirmation.
  • Uninstall the app — everything it stored goes with it. On iPhone, photos are excluded from iCloud backup, so no copy survives elsewhere.

On an Android phone the steps are slightly different; see Android (Google Play) below.

On our servers

Nothing is filed under you, because nothing identifies you. Photos and profile data are not retained once an analysis is answered. Service logs carry no photo, no profile and no user identifier.

Held by others

  • The subscription service holds an anonymous purchase identifier, created on first launch so the store can recognise your subscription. To have it deleted, send a message from Give Feedback in Settings with the subject “Delete my purchase identifier”; the identifier is attached automatically. We delete it and confirm by email within 30 days.
  • Apple and Google keep purchase records under their own accounting and tax obligations; we cannot remove those.

Android (Google Play)

FitMax for Android on Google Play — package com.fitmax.sameh — has no account either and keeps everything on the phone.

  • In the app: open Profile, tap the gear icon, choose Delete my data and confirm. Every analysis with its photos, your profile, your programmes, your workout log, personal records and favourites are removed from the phone in one step.
  • Uninstall the app: Android removes everything the app stored. The app takes no part in Google's cloud backup, so no copy is kept in your Google account, and photos and reports are never carried to a new phone by device-to-device transfer.
  • The anonymous purchase identifier: send Give Feedback from the same settings screen with the subject “Delete my purchase identifier”; the identifier is attached automatically. If the app is no longer installed, email mehmetsancaktutannn3@gmail.com with that subject and the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.) so the purchase can be found. We delete it and confirm by email within 30 days.

What the in-app deletion keeps. Your subscription status, the anonymous purchase identifier and a few technical settings stay on the phone, so that a paying subscriber is not asked to pay again. None of them contains a photo, a measurement or your profile, and uninstalling the app removes them too.

What others keep. Google Play keeps your purchase and order records under Google's own accounting and tax obligations. The diagnostics and installation identifier that Google's ML Kit sends to Google contain no photo or profile data; Google handles them under its own policies, and we cannot delete them for you.

Deletion on the phone is permanent. There is no cloud copy to restore from, because there was never a cloud copy. And deleting your data does not cancel your subscription — if you only want to stop paying, cancel it in the store instead.
