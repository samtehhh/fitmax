Terms of Use

Last updated 21 September 2026

What the app does

FitMax analyses front, side and back photographs of your body. It scores the physique out of 100 on several axes, estimates body-fat percentage, scores six muscle groups, writes a training programme from the result, and keeps a log of your workouts and of every report so that analyses can be compared over time.

Estimates, not measurements

Every number the app produces is an estimate. It rests on what the camera could see — pose, light, clothing and distance all move it — and on a fixed scoring method applied the same way to every photo. A fixed method applied to a photograph still produces an estimate, and two analyses of the same body under different conditions can differ. The body-fat figure in particular is not a measurement.

FitMax is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing in a report or a programme is medical advice. If you have a medical condition, an injury, an eating disorder or are pregnant, talk to a doctor before following a programme or changing your diet because of a score.

You must be 18 or older, and every photo you submit must be of yourself. Do not submit photographs of anyone else. You are responsible for the profile you enter: a wrong height or weight makes every estimate built on it wrong.

Your photos

Your photos remain yours. You grant us only what is needed to run the analysis you asked for: sending the photo to the AI provider and returning the result. We do not keep them, publish them or train anything on them.
