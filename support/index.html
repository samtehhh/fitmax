Support

Write to mehmetsancaktutannn3@gmail.com — a person reads it. Use Give Feedback in Settings: the message includes your anonymous purchase identifier, which is the only way to find your purchase when there is no account.

"We couldn't see your body clearly"

The analysis needs the whole body in frame, from head to feet, in fitted clothes and even light. Stand a few steps from the camera, avoid mirrors and strong backlight, and take all three angles — front, side and back — the same way. A cropped or dark photo is refused rather than guessed at.

The scores look too low, or too high

Every score is an estimate from a photograph. Pose, light, clothing and distance all move it, so compare analyses taken under the same conditions. The scoring method is fixed and encouraging by design; it is a starting point for training, not a verdict.

Why does the analysis ask me to subscribe?

Every analysis and every generated programme is a paid request to an external processing service, so those are part of FitMax Premium. The app shows the price and period before you buy anything. The exercise library, workout logging and your saved reports stay open without a subscription.

I subscribed, but the app still asks me to pay

Use Restore purchases in Settings. Store confirmation can take a moment to reach the app. If it persists, write to us via Give Feedback in Settings. On Android, follow the steps under Android (Google Play).

Cancel a subscription, or get a refund

Both are handled by the store, not by us: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions (see Android (Google Play)). Cancelling stops the next renewal and does not refund the current period. Deleting your data does not cancel the subscription.

The report is in the wrong language

A report is written in the language the app was set to when you ran it, and a saved report keeps that language. Change the app language and run a new analysis to get one in the new language.

My old reports are gone

Reports and photos live only on the phone: on iPhone they are excluded from iCloud backup on purpose, on Android the app takes no part in Google's cloud backup, and there is no cloud copy. Deleting the app, or using Delete my data in Settings, removes them permanently.

Android (Google Play)

FitMax for Android is available on Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitmax.sameh. Give Feedback works the same way there and attaches your anonymous purchase identifier.

Restore a subscription

  1. Make sure the Play Store on this phone is signed in to the Google account you subscribed with.
  2. In FitMax, open Profile, tap the gear icon, then Restore purchases.
  3. If Google Play shows the payment as pending — for example a cash payment, or one waiting for approval in a family group — Premium opens as soon as Google confirms it.

Manage or cancel

Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FitMax. There you can cancel or update the payment method. On a computer, use play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions with the same Google account. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Google decides refunds under the Google Play refund policies; we cannot issue them. Request one from your Google Play order history: play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.

Delete my data

Delete your data — on the phone, or by email for the anonymous purchase identifier.
